## Confirmed user rules — updated 2026-10-19 (OVERRIDES earlier calendar-month/threshold-only assumptions)

- **Anniversary billing cycle:** use the trusted server signup-request Bangkok date as the anchor. Signup **16/10/2026**: cycle 1 is **16/10–15/11**, reset **16/11** at 00:00 Asia/Bangkok; cycle 2 is 16/11–15/12, reset 16/12. The end boundary is exclusive. 29–31 signup dates clamp to the last day of shorter months and restore the original day in later longer months.
- Only **one fee model per anniversary cycle**: fixed fee if paid Delivery merchandise stays below configurable threshold when cycle closes; otherwise share percentage only. First **anniversary cycle** waives only fixed fee. POS/Table/Takeaway/shipping excluded.
- **Trigger-day whole-day rule:** when cumulative paid Delivery reaches threshold at any hour (e.g. 09:00), wait until **that Bangkok calendar day finishes** and bill share on **ALL paid Delivery merchandise sales from that day**. Example threshold 10,000, 09:00 cumulative 10,000, 23:00 today's total 15,500: **15,500 × 5% = 775 THB**. Prior days' sales are NOT retroactively shared. Following days charge share only on each day's paid Delivery sales.
- Share invoice for service day D is calculated/issued after day D closes; unpaid share suspends operations from D+1. Uploading a slip is not payment confirmation. Confirmed overdue debt persists across anniversary resets.
- `monthlySlipQuota` counts per **anniversary billing cycle**, resets with revenue at signup-day boundary, and entitlement becomes unlimited after reaching the threshold in that cycle (provider technical limits still apply).
- Four Super Admin configurable parameters: `monthlyFee`, `deliveryThreshold`, `deliveryShareRate`, `monthlySlipQuota`. Money in satang, timestamp and day Asia/Bangkok, immutable cycle snapshot and invoice IDs.
- Existing Subscription/Revenue Share tenants are unaffected. Billing signup remains **disabled**, ledger/payment/quota engines not exported and **no production deploy**.
